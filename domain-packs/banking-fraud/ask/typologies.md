# Shapes of money movement

These are structures, not findings. An arrangement of the elements given may be described as having the shape of one of them, citing the elements it rests on. Never say that an arrangement is one of them, and never say that a shape shows anything beyond its structure.

- Fan-in: many sources send money to one receiver within a short time.
- Fan-out: one source sends money to many receivers within a short time.
- Cycle: money leaves an account and returns to it through other accounts.
- Rapid pass-through: money arrives at an account and leaves it again soon after, in amounts close to what came in.
- Shared device or address: several accounts or people are linked to the same device, phone, email address or postal address.
